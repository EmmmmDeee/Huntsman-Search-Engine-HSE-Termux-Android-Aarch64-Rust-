Radar

Reads Android's last cached Wi-Fi scan and the cells it reports through Termux:API; nothing here triggers an active scan. No root or provider key required. Observations may be old, and signal strength does not establish a device's location. Log a sweep now and then as you move to notice a device that moves with you.

No observations requested. Termux:API and Android location permission are required on the phone.

About


Huntsman is an OSINT engine in one Rust binary that runs on Android in Termux without root. Scans, their data elements and their logs stay on this device.
