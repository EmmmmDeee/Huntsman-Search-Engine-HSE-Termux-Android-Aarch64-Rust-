# Revenue playbook

This document converts current, demonstrated Huntsman capabilities into small,
sellable research services without claiming product maturity or legacy parity.
It is an execution guide, not a revenue claim.

## Economic rule

Prioritise work by expected net value:

```text
expected net value =
    expected cash upside
  - direct cash cost
  - expected loss / rework
  - opportunity cost
  - skilled-labour time cost
```

Prefer, in order:

1. recurring paid research;
2. repeatable fixed-scope research packages;
3. reusable report/data assets;
4. productised recurring monitoring;
5. software licensing only after stable-product gates are met.

## Current sellable surface

Use only capabilities that the current tree can demonstrate:

- open-web research and source validation;
- structured evidence trails and provenance;
- entity and selector classification;
- public-source company, domain, email, username and phone research where the
  relevant current command path exists;
- deterministic local processing;
- bounded public-data collection;
- JSON, Markdown and tabular delivery;
- Rust CLI/data-tooling work backed by this repository as a public portfolio.

Do not market historical legacy modules as current capability. Do not promise
full automated investigations, complete coverage, production SLOs, or a stable
public API until those gates are demonstrated.

## Offer 1 — Evidence-backed research sprint

**Target buyer:** founders, analysts, journalists, researchers, AI evaluation
teams, consultants and small due-diligence practices.

**Pilot price:** USD 150-300 fixed price.

**Scope:** one clearly bounded research question, organisation, product claim,
public event, domain, or factual timeline.

**Deliverable:**

- concise answer;
- source table with exact URLs and access dates;
- fact / inference / unknown separation;
- contradictions and unresolved gaps;
- optional JSON export;
- reproducible search notes;
- explicit confidence and invalidation conditions.

**Acceptance:** every material conclusion must map to inspectable public
evidence. Unsupported conclusions are labelled unknown.

**Scale path:** convert repeated requests into a reusable source pack,
collector, report generator, or monitoring workflow.

## Offer 2 — Public-source company / counterparty research

**Pilot price:** USD 250-500 fixed price for a bounded entity and jurisdiction.

**Deliverable:**

- legal / public identity summary from available public records;
- corporate and web-presence timeline where supported;
- material public claims and contradictions;
- adverse-media or reputation findings where relevant and lawful;
- source provenance;
- unresolved identity-resolution questions;
- machine-readable evidence appendix.

This is research, not a regulated legal, financial, licensing, credit, or
private-investigator determination. Jurisdiction-specific regulated work is
accepted only where the operator is authorised to provide it.

## Offer 3 — AI browsing benchmark research

**Target buyer:** teams building or evaluating browsing agents.

**Commercial model:** hourly or per accepted benchmark item.

**Deliverable per item:**

- objectively verifiable answer;
- difficult natural-language research question;
- independently checkable clues;
- primary-source evidence trail;
- validation searches;
- ambiguity review;
- structured JSON record.

This offer has direct market fit with current 2026 contractor demand for
investigative web research, evidence tracing and benchmark construction.

## Offer 4 — Rust research / data tooling

**Target buyer:** engineering teams needing bounded Rust CLI, data-processing,
validation, or research automation work.

**Initial commercial model:** fixed-scope paid prototype or hourly contract.

Use the Huntsman repository as proof of Rust systems work, deterministic
processing, CLI design, HTTP integrations, CI/release machinery and Android
aarch64 delivery. Do not claim experience or production scale that the
repository does not demonstrate.

## Pricing experiment

The first objective is paid validation, not maximum nominal rate.

Run three price points for comparable fixed-scope work:

| Variant | Price | Purpose |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Entry pilot | USD 150 | minimise purchase friction and obtain first receipt |
| Standard sprint | USD 300 | test sustainable research economics |
| Extended sprint | USD 500 | test higher-value, deeper evidence work |

Track:

- proposals sent;
- replies;
- paid conversions;
- gross cash;
- platform fees / direct costs;
- skilled labour hours;
- cash per skilled labour hour;
- revisions;
- reusable assets created;
- follow-on work.

Raise price when conversion remains acceptable and delivery time is stable.
Reduce scope before reducing evidence quality.

## Immediate operating sequence

1. Apply to the highest-fit current paid research opportunity.
2. Offer a tightly bounded paid pilot rather than an open-ended investigation.
3. Deliver with the report template in `docs/CLIENT_RESEARCH_REPORT.md`.
4. Record actual hours, cash and revisions.
5. Extract every reusable source list, parser, schema and report component.
6. Re-price from observed delivery economics.
7. Productise only repeated, proven work.
