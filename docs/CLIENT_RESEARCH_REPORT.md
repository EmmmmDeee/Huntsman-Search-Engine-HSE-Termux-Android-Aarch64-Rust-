inference and unknowns.

## Evidence

For every material finding record:

- exact source URL;
- source title / publisher;
- publication or record date where available;
- access date;
- exact page, section, table or record identifier when available;
- what the source proves;
- what it does not prove.

## Contradictions

Record material conflicts between credible sources and explain which conclusion,
if any, survives.

## Gaps and unknowns

List unresolved questions. Do not convert missing evidence into a negative
finding.

## Reproducibility

Record the search terms, source classes, public registries and other material
steps needed for another researcher to reproduce the result.

## Machine-readable appendix

Where useful, provide the same findings as JSON with stable field names for
entity, claim, status, confidence, source, access date, lineage and notes.

## Delivery acceptance

A report is complete only when:

- every material conclusion maps to inspectable evidence;
- unsupported claims are labelled inference or unknown;
- contradictory evidence is preserved;
- source provenance is retained;
- no planned Huntsman capability is represented as demonstrated current
  capability.
