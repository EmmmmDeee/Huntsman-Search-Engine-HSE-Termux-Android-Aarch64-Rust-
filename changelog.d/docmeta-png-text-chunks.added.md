- `docmeta` now reads a PNG's uncompressed textual chunks (`tEXt` and `iTXt`) and modification time (`tIME`) as `png:*` fields, surfacing embedded author, description, copyright, software, comment and timestamp metadata that was previously dropped. Compressed chunks (`zTXt`, compressed `iTXt`) are not inflated and the binary `eXIf` block is left to the `exif` command, keeping the keyless, offline, no-decompression contract.
