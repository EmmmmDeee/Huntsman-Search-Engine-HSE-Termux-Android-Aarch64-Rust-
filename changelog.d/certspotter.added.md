- `domain` (and `scan -k domain`, `sf -s` and the web UI) reads every certificate Cert Spotter logged for the domain and its subdomains, as Spiderfoot's `sfp_certspotter` does but keyless (`HUNTSMAN_CERTSPOTTER_KEY` raises the limit). Each certificate is a record on the domain with Cert Spotter's hashes, issuer, validity, revocation and names, and from the certificate itself its serial, subject, key and signature algorithms and the PEM: `SSL_CERTIFICATE_RAW`, `SSL_CERTIFICATE_ISSUED`, `SSL_CERTIFICATE_ISSUER` and `SSL_CERTIFICATE_EXPIRING`/`_EXPIRED` in a saved scan. Every name is a domain entity listing its certificates, and an OV or EV certificate's organisation is an entity with its place and registration number. `x509` is a dependency-free certificate reader and `base64` gains a decoder.
