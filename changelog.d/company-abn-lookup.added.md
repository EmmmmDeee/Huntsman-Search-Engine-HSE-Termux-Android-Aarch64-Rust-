- `company ABN|ACN` now also asks the Australian Business Register through ABN Lookup when `HUNTSMAN_ABR_GUID` holds the operator's free ABR web-services GUID, registered at abr.business.gov.au/Tools/WebServices. A match adds the registered entity name and type, ABN status and effective date, GST registration, state and postcode, and every business name, as `registry: ABR` evidence. The ABR answers an unrecognised GUID with HTTP 200 and a message, so that answer is reported as a rejected key, never as a missing record. A record must name exactly the number asked. Without the GUID the leg is not configured and sends nothing.
