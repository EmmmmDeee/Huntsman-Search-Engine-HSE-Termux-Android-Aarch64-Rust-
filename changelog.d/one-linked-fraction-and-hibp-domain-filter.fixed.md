- `scan --recurse` prints one linked fraction, not two that disagree. The `summary` line's `linked_fraction` (and `metrics.linked_entity_fraction` in JSON) counted an entity whose only relations were self-loops, or edges to an entity outside the snapshot such as a truncated crawl's lineage links, as linked, and counted a repeated uid twice. The `gaps` line counted neither. Both now count an entity as linked when it has an edge to another entity, over distinct entities.
- The HIBP client trims a breach-list or breached-account domain filter and refuses a blank one (`domain is empty`) before sending anything. It used to send `Domain=%20`. The `hibp` command behaves as before; only its own copy of that check is gone.
