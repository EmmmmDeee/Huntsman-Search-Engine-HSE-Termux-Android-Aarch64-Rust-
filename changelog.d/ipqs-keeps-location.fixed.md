- An `ip` lookup with `HUNTSMAN_IPQS_KEY` set keeps where IPQualityScore places the address. IPQS answers with the region, postcode, time zone, latitude and longitude, and whether a VPN or Tor exit is active now, and `ipqs::decode` kept only the city and the standing VPN/Tor flags. The position is now a coordinates entity of the lookup (also `scan -k ip` and the web UI's IP lookup), tagged `geoint`; the region, postcode and time zone are on the address's IPQS evidence, and `active_vpn`/`active_tor` join its flags. IPQS's `N/A` is not kept as a postcode.
