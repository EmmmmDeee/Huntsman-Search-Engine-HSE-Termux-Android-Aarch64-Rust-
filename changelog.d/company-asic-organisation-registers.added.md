- New `company NAME|ACN|ABN` command: one Australian organisation looked up on three keyless ASIC registers on data.gov.au. The Company Dataset holds 4.46 million companies. Each match is kept under its current name, with any former name, its ACN, ABN, type, class, status and registration dates. Business Names holds 3.30 million trading names, each linked to its holder by ABN. Banned and Disqualified Organisations entries are tagged as regulatory actions. A NAME is also looked up on GLEIF. A name matches only a record holding every query word, and an ACN or ABN must pass its checksum and match exactly. A register that holds no such number is not asked. Every match is a register entry to review, not a confirmed identity.
- The CKAN register collectors (`asic_persons`, `acnc` and the new `asic_orgs`) now share one fail-closed datastore query path in `ckan`, replacing two identical copies.
