- A `people NAME` lookup now also searches four more keyless ASIC registers on data.gov.au: registered liquidators, registered company auditors, approved SMSF auditors and AFS authorised representatives. Each match keeps its registration number, status, dates, conditions and licence numbers verbatim. A listed firm becomes an organisation lead, a checksum-valid ABN or ACN becomes a pivot, and a registered address carries its postcode centroid. A company-named holder is an organisation, never a person. A register listing is a licence record, never an adverse finding. People-scan coverage now counts twelve sources. A scan saved before this change shows the four new registers as not observed, never as clean zeros.
