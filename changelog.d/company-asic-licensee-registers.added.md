- `company NAME|ACN|ABN` now also searches ASIC's AFS licensee and credit licensee registers on data.gov.au, keyless. A match keeps its licence number, start and end dates, status, authorisations, linked AFS licence and business names verbatim. It adds the licensee's ABN or ACN as a pivot and its registered address with a postcode centroid. A licence is a permission ASIC granted, never a finding. Because each record is keyed by exact number, one ABN joins a company, its business names and its licences into one organisation.
