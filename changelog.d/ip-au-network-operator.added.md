- `ip` says when the address's origin AS belongs to an Australian consumer internet provider
  (Telstra, Optus, TPG, iiNet, Aussie Broadband and others) or to AARNet. It reads RIPEstat's
  holder (`ASN-TELSTRA - Telstra Limited`) and records the operator on the address with an
  `au-consumer-isp` or `au-academic-network` tag. A common word such as "belong" names an
  operator only in the holder's handle, never in its free-text name. The tag describes the
  operator's market; it does not prove that the address is a home connection.
