- `domain` and `ip` lookups, and so scans, `sf -s` and the web UI, read the registration over RDAP, keyless, from the registry the IANA bootstrap names, as Spiderfoot's `sfp_whois` reads WHOIS. The subject keeps the whole record as published (a saved scan's `DOMAIN_WHOIS` or `NETBLOCK_WHOIS`), its registrar (`DOMAIN_REGISTRAR`), every event date, status, name server and network block (`NETBLOCK_MEMBER`), and every contact's handle, name, organisation, kind, address, telephone and email under `<role>_<field>`. The registrar, each name server, contact organisation, email and telephone is an entity tied to the subject (abuse contacts tagged `abuse-contact`); a registry's privacy placeholder is kept in the record but never becomes an entity. A host is looked up as its registrable domain, a 404 is a validated zero, and a TLD with no RDAP service says so on the `rdap` row.
