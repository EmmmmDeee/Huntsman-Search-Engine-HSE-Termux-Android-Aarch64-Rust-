- An `email`, `username` or `people` lookup keeps what its keyed legs found when its own sources fail. SeekNow (with `HUNTSMAN_SEEKNOW_KEY`) and, for an address or a handle, the breach and exposure legs (HIBP, OathNet and DeHashed, each with its key) run beside the lookup's own sources. When every one of those sources failed, or the network refused them, the lookup was refused (exit 69 or 77) and whatever the keyed legs had found was dropped, though they had run. Now a lookup where any keyed leg answered reports that leg's findings and rows, saved as an exposure when a breach leg's answer is folded in, and one `email=unavailable reason=…` line (`username=…`, `people=…`) says why the lookup's own sources gave nothing. A lookup where no leg answered is refused as before, with its own reason and exit status. Five tests in `src/cli/lookup/profiles.rs` cover the email, username and people lookups, the breach legs and the refusal; the first four fail on the previous code.
