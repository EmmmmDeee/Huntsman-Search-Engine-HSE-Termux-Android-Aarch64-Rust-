- The Wi-Fi Radar table now shows every field the OS cache gives for a network: its exact frequency in MHz, its channel width, its security capabilities (`[WPA2-PSK-CCMP][ESS]`, or an open network's absence of them) and the OS clock reading the age is computed from, beside the coarse band, age and freshness it already showed. Before, these observations were recorded and reached only the exported JSON, so telling two same-named networks apart or noting an open network meant downloading and opening a file on the phone.
