- Add a keyless Keybase identity source to the `username` lookup (and any scan that reaches the username collectors). Keybase links a handle to the external accounts, websites and PGP key its owner has cryptographically proven they control, so a lookup now surfaces those linked identities: the proven accounts as usernames and profile URLs, DNS- and website-verified domains as controlled domains, the owner's full name, and the primary PGP key fingerprint as a pivot. A handle that cannot name a Keybase username is inconclusive without a request, and an absent or null account is a clean negative.
