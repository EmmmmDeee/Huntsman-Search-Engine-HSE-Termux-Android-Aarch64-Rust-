- A running scan now saves what it has found after each wave of lookups, so a serve process that Android stops mid-scan (Termux swiped away, the phantom-process killer, low memory) leaves an `ABORTED` scan that still shows every element, source outcome and report line it had gathered, and the Scans list counts elements while a scan runs. Before, nothing was written until the whole crawl finished, so a killed depth-1 or depth-2 scan recovered with "No data" and every lookup had to be re-run and re-spent. The recovery log line now says how many data elements were kept, or that the scan had saved nothing yet.
