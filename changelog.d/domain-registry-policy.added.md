- `domain` notes, offline, what the name's registry requires of whoever holds a name in its
  namespace: a `.gov.au` name is held by an Australian government body (and `nsw.gov.au` by a
  New South Wales one), an `.id.au` name by a natural person, an `.edu.vn` name by an education
  institution, and so on. It is recorded as `registry_policy` evidence and a `registrant:<class>`
  tag on the domain. It describes the namespace, never who holds the name, and it never turns a
  lookup that no source answered into a result.
