- Netlas reports what it found the right way round on a `domain` or `ip` lookup. A domain Netlas has never scanned was a success with an empty finding (`Netlas: 0 open port(s), 0 IOC listing(s)`) and a 404 was a valid zero, but Netlas answers an unscanned domain with 200 and only its name, and gives a 404 only for a path it does not have (checked 2026-10-15).
