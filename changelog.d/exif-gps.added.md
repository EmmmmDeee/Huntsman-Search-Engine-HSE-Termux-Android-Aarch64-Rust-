- `exif IMAGE-FILE`: keyless, offline extraction of the EXIF metadata a camera embeds in a local image. JPEG and TIFF images are parsed; IFD0, the Exif sub-IFD and the GPS IFD are decoded and every tag is kept as evidence on a document entity, so camera make and model, lens, timestamps, orientation and dimensions are preserved. When the GPS IFD holds a position it also becomes a coordinates entity -- the same `lat,lon` the `geocode` command reverse-geocodes to an address -- with altitude and GPS timestamp when present. EXIF is editable, so the position is the file's embedded claim, not an independently verified location. The parser is bounds-checked, allocation-bounded and dependency-free.
