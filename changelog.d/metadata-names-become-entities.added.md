- A person or organisation named in a file's metadata becomes an entity of its own, attributed to
  the file and the field: the EXIF artist, author (Windows' `XPAuthor`), camera owner,
  photographer and editor, and the XMP, PDF, PNG and IPTC fields that name someone:
  - `dc:creator` and `dc:contributor`;
  - the PDF `/Info` Author, including one an update superseded;
  - the PNG `Author`;
  - the IPTC by-line and caption writer;
  - the rights and licence owners;
  - a person shown, and a face region's name.

  A list in one field gives one entity per name. A name must be written as a name: letters and
  the punctuation of initials and company names. A copyright line, a handle, a single word and a
  placeholder a program writes for an unknown user ("Microsoft Office User", "Windows User",
  "Preferred Customer") do not count. The same name in several fields or both readers is one
  entity with each field as evidence.
