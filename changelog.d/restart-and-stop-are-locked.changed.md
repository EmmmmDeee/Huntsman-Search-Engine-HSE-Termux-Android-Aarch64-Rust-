- Restart recovery and Stop are now locked where the phone meets them, not only in the store's own tests: an integration test starts `serve` over scans a previous process left `RUNNING` (one with no owner recorded, one whose owner's pid is gone) and reads them `ABORTED` with the reason in their log; the scan API's Stop on a running scan is exercised to its 202 answer and the scan's settling `ABORTED`; the page's list of live statuses is checked against the store's, so Stop is offered exactly while a scan can be stopped and polling runs on through `ABORT-REQUESTED`; and the phone-browser check plants a left-running scan before its restart and expects the row to read `ABORTED` and offer Delete. Before, dropping the start-up sweep or changing the Stop route's answer left a scan reading `RUNNING` for ever on the page, with Stop refused and Delete hidden, and nothing failed.
