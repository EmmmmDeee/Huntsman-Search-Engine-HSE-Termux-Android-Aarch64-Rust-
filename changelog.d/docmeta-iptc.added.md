- `docmeta` now reads a JPEG's IPTC IIM metadata (the legacy Photoshop `APP13` record), surfacing the by-line and caption writer, credit and source, headline and caption, keywords, and the named location (city, sub-location, province, country) as `iptc:*` fields, keyless and offline. This complements the XMP packet and the EXIF block, which many files leave empty while still carrying IPTC.
