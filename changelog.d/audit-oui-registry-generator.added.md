- `cargo run --locked -p xtask -- oui pack oui.csv src/oui_ieee.bin` builds the embedded IEEE MA-L registry from the registry's CSV (<https://standards-oui.ieee.org/oui/oui.csv>), and `oui dump` lists a blob. The 762 KB blob had no generator in the repository, so it could not be refreshed or audited; `xtask/tests/oui_pack.rs` now proves the committed blob is what the packer makes of its own listing, byte for byte, and the first of a duplicate assignment is kept and reported. The blob is refreshed from the registry published on 2026-10-14: 40,333 assignments (858 more than before) from 20,125 organisations, with the registry's current name on 228 assignments it had renamed; the binary grows by 7 KB.
