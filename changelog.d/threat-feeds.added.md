Each is downloaded whole and kept in `$HUNTSMAN_HOME/feeds`, else `~/.huntsman/feeds`, for as long as Spiderfoot keeps it, read from its last copy when it cannot be downloaded again, and reported as changed form when it holds nothing readable. Every listing keeps the feed's whole entry and is `BLACKLISTED_*` and `MALICIOUS_*` in a saved scan (Steven Black's list `BLACKLISTED_*` only; a Tor exit relay `TOR_EXIT_NODE`). A netblock's malicious listing is now `MALICIOUS_NETBLOCK`.
