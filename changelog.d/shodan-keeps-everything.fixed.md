- An `ip` or `domain` lookup with `HUNTSMAN_SHODAN_KEY` keeps everything Shodan says. An address was asked for with `minify=true`, which leaves out every service banner, and the decoder dropped the position, region, country name, postcode and area code. Shodan is now asked for the whole host. Every banner is kept whole in `raw_banners`, and read out of them are the services with product and version, CPEs, crawler modules, HTTP titles, servers, redirects, components, WAF and favicon hashes, TLS subjects, issuers, expiry, fingerprints, versions, JARM and JA3S, SSH fingerprints and HASSH, device types, cloud, each CVE with its CVSS score, the known-exploited ones and the highest score. The rest of the answer is kept in `raw_host`, the position is `coordinates` (an entity when Shodan names the city), and TLS subject names are domain pivots. When the whole answer is longer than the body limit, the lookup asks again for the minified answer and says so in `banners_omitted`, where it used to fail. A domain keeps every DNS record as `name TYPE value` (TXT and every other type), its subdomains, the newest `last_seen` and the whole answer in `raw_answer`. `api_leg` reports an answer past the body limit as `LegError::TooLarge`.
