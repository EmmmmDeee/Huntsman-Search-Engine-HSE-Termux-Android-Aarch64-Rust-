- The web UI's Analyze tab gains an **Extract seeds** control: paste an advert, a message thread or a page, and every email, phone, handle, domain, URL, IP, coordinates and company number in it joins the seeds, once each, listed with the classifier signal that found it. It runs `POST /api/investigate`, the `investigate` command's extraction over the text sent as the body (never a URL), answered as a report like a lookup's with no outcomes, guarded like a lookup and bounded like a form body (a longer text is answered 413 naming `investigate --file FILE`). Before, the command ran only from a Termux shell: pasting prose into Analyze classified each line as one seed and rejected it, and more than 16 lines were refused outright.
