- A Wikidata person who holds or held a public office is tagged `politically-exposed`: the positions
  held (P39) are classified with one bounded request for their records, a position counting only
  when typed as a public or elective office or a subclass of head of state, head of government,
  minister, prime minister, emir, legislator, member of parliament or mayor (the generic
  "position" type a company president also carries does not count). Each office is kept as
  current or former (an end time makes it former). A failed request only records why.
