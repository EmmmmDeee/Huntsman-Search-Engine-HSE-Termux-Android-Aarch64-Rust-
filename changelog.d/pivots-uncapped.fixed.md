- A lookup keeps every other indicator a source names. `Finding::pivot` dropped everything past the fiftieth pivot of a finding (`MAX_PIVOTS`), for all eighteen indicator sources, with nothing in the result to say so. A domain with eighty subdomains in SecurityTrails got fifty subdomain entities, and urlscan.io's hundred scans of `example.com` named fifty of their hosts and addresses. Every pivot is now kept, and a duplicate is found through a set rather than a scan of the list, so a finding with thousands of pivots builds in linear time.
