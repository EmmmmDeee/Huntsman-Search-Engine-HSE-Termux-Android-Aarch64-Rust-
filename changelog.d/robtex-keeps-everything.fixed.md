- An `ip` or `domain` lookup keeps everything Robtex says. `robtex::decode_ip` kept at most ten passive DNS names, and never the city, the AS and WHOIS descriptions, the route description, the active DNS names or when any name was seen. `robtex::decode_forward` kept at most ten addresses and ten names and skipped every TXT and other record. Every name, address and record is now kept, with the first and newest sightings. An address's whole answer is kept as JSON in `raw_answer`, and a domain's records in `raw_records`. Every passive and active name is a domain pivot.
