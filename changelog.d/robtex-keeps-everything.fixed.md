- An `ip` or `domain` lookup keeps everything Robtex says. `robtex::decode_ip` kept at most ten passive DNS names, and never the city, the AS and WHOIS descriptions, the route description, the active DNS names or the time Robtex dates them by. `robtex::decode_forward` kept at most ten addresses and ten names and skipped every TXT and other record. Every name, address and record is now kept, with a domain's first and newest sightings. An address's whole answer is kept as JSON in `raw_answer`, and a domain's records in `raw_records`. Every passive and active name is a domain pivot.
