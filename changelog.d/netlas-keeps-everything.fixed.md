- An `ip` or `domain` lookup keeps everything Netlas holds. `netlas::decode` kept at most fifteen protocols, programs, threats and IOC tags, and by design never decoded WHOIS contacts. It also dropped the organisation, anycast and registered country, the AS registry, country and CIDR, the network's organisation, handle, description, address, emails and phones, the abuse mailbox, port transports, software categories and URIs, IOC URLs, first sightings, scores and notes, DNS TXT records, and a domain's whole WHOIS registration. The whole answer is now kept as JSON in `raw_answer`, WHOIS text included. Every one of those values is read out, along with the registrar, the registration dates and status, and the registrant's organisation, country and email. An anycast address is tagged `anycast`.
