- `huntsman-recon doctor` and `doctor serve` now probe the address `huntsman start` recorded in `~/.huntsman/serve.bind` (or `$HUNTSMAN_HOME/serve.bind`) when no `--bind` or `HSE_BIND` is given, so a phone whose service moved off a taken port 8080 is reported as running instead of "not running on 127.0.0.1:8080". The report and `doctor serve` say where the address came from (`bind=`), the serve check names the recorded file, and under the Termux service its fixes are `huntsman start` and `huntsman restart` rather than a `huntsman-recon serve` that would fight the service. A recorded line that is not `HOST:PORT` is a configuration error (exit 78) naming the file.
