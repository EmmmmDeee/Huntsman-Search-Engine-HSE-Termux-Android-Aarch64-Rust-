- The Android binary is linked for 16 KiB pages (`-z max-page-size=16384`), which devices with 16 KiB pages (Android 15 and later on recent hardware) require to load it at all, and both CI cross-builds refuse a binary whose LOAD segments are aligned to less. Until now the alignment was whatever the runner's NDK gave: r28 links for 16 KiB by default and the binary published today is aligned, but r27, the documented reference, links for 4 KiB, and nothing checked. The provenance file records `max_page_size`.
