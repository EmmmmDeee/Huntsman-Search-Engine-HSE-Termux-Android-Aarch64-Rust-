- `serve`'s continuous collection can now keep its own store under capacity: set `HUNTSMAN_CONTINUOUS_KEEP=N` (1 to 4096) and each cycle, once its alert decisions are recorded, deletes the configured targets' ended scans beyond the newest N, as `scans prune --keep N --apply` would. Scans of targets that are not configured are never touched, a cycle whose alert decisions could not be recorded deletes nothing, and a wrong value stops `serve` before it binds. Retention, by `scans prune` or by the setting, now never deletes a target's newest finished scan, however many failed or aborted scans followed it, so the last complete evidence and the next alert's baseline survive.
