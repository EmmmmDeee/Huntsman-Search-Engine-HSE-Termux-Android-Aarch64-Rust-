- AU-109 `shared_registrant` no longer names domains as one registrant's or operator's when all they share is a privacy service or a provider's own mailbox. Two domains under registries that publish registrant contacts, such as `alpha-shop.org` and `beta-shop.info` both registered through "Domains By Proxy, LLC", each got a `RegisteredBy` relation from their RDAP record to the proxy's organisation, name or mailbox (`alpha-shop-org@domainsbyproxy.com`, which names the service), so `scan --recurse`, web UI scans and stored-scan correlation reported them as sharing one registrant. The same happened through a registrant mailbox at a registrar's or host's own mail domain (`hostmaster@godaddy.com`), through Pulsedive's WHOIS `organisation` naming the proxy (a `SameOperator` link to it), and when three or more domains shared a proxy name such as "Domain Protection Services, Inc." as their `registrant_org`. None of these gives a relation now; the value stays in each domain's evidence and the proxy stays an entity. Whoxy lookups already worked this way.
