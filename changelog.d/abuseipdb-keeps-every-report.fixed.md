- AbuseIPDB (with `HUNTSMAN_ABUSEIPDB_KEY`) asks for the report list (`verbose`) and keeps every report on the address: when, the abuse categories by name, the reporter's id and country and their comment, plus a count per category, the country name, whether the address is public, its IP version and the whole answer as `raw_answer`. When the answer with every report is past the body limit, the check is asked again without them and marked `reports_unread`, so the score is never lost.
