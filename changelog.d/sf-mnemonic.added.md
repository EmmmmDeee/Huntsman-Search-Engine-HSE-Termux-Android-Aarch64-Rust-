- `ip` and `domain` read Mnemonic's passive DNS, keyless (`mnemonic`, Spiderfoot's `sfp_mnemonic`): every record seen for the domain or address, up to 1,000, kept as published with first and last seen, count and TTL range. A domain's addresses, names and the names its CNAME, MX and NS records point to, and an address's co-hosted sites, become entities. Records Mnemonic leaves without a last-seen time are kept with the dates it created and updated them, where Spiderfoot drops them.
