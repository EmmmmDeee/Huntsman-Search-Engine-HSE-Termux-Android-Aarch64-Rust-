- Merging two records of one source about one entity keeps every value whole and in the order it was observed. When `merge_by_uid` folded two such records (the same address looked up on two days and loaded together, or met again in each recursion generation), `merge_evidence` split every differing attribute on `; `, sorted the pieces and joined them again. A value that holds `; ` in its own text, such as an AbuseIPDB report comment `Port scan; SSH brute force (22/tcp)`, came back cut into pieces and shuffled among the other record's pieces, and listed values such as ports came back sorted as text (`22; 443; 8080`) instead of in the order they were seen. A merge now keeps each record's value whole, the earlier one first, and writes once the values where one record continues the other's list. `Evidence::with_attr` also no longer adds a second copy of a value that holds `; `.
