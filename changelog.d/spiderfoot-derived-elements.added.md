- Saved scans now carry the elements Spiderfoot's own modules derive. As `sfp_dnsresolve` does, each host's domain is reported once: the target's as `DOMAIN_NAME` (`DOMAIN_NAME_PARENT` when the target sits below it), an affiliate's as `AFFILIATE_DOMAIN_NAME` and a co-hosted site's as `CO_HOSTED_SITE_DOMAIN`. As `sfp_countryname` does, each country a phone number's dialling code, a domain's TLD, an IBAN, a place, an address or a WHOIS record names becomes a `COUNTRY_NAME` element under it, in Spiderfoot's own country names (`sf_country`). Spiderfoot's `outlier_country` correlation rule now fires on real scans. The text reading also does three things Spiderfoot's pattern means to and misses: a country at the start or end of the text, a name closed by `)` or `]`, and the whole value after `Country:`.
