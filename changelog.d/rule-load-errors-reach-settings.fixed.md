- A Spiderfoot-format correlation rule in `~/.huntsman/correlations` that could not be read or loaded is now named on the web UI's Settings panel, under Correlation Rules, with the reason, and `/api/correlationrules` and `scans rules --json` carry it as a row whose origin is `not loaded`. Before, `serve` printed the failure to its own standard error only, which the Termux service sends to its log, so the panel listed every other rule and gave no sign on the phone that one was silently not applied to any scan.
