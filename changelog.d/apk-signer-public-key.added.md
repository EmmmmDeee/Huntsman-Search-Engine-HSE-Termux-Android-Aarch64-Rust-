- `apk FILE` now reports each signing certificate's **public-key digest** — the SHA-256 of its `SubjectPublicKeyInfo` — alongside the certificate digest, the same pair `apksigner --print-certs` prints. Google Play keys an app's signing identity and its key-rotation lineage on the public key, not the whole certificate, so this pivots across certificates that re-wrap the same key (a renewed certificate, Play app-signing) where the per-certificate digest cannot. Reported as `analysis.signers[].public_key_sha256` in the JSON and `apk_signer_public_key_sha256_unverified` on the document evidence, carried as a `public_key_sha256` attribute on the signer entity, and emitted as a distinct `apk-signer-pubkey-sha256:<digest>` pivot entity (one per distinct key). The `SubjectPublicKeyInfo` element is read from the certificate DER the signer parser already walks; no signature is verified and a matching key is a candidate lead, not proof of a valid signature. No new dependency.
