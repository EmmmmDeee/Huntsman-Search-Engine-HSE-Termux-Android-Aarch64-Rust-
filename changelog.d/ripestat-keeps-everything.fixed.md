- An `ip` lookup keeps everything RIPEstat says about the address. `ripestat::decode` kept at most eight origin AS numbers, and never the related prefixes, the `see_also` prefixes, the registry block's resource and registry name, whether the prefix was aligned to a less-specific one, or RIPEstat's messages. An address no AS announces was a valid zero even though RIPEstat named the registry block that holds it. Every origin AS is now kept, as an entity with its holder. The address evidence carries `related_prefixes`, `see_also`, `block_resource`, `block_registry`, `announced`, `less_specific`, `messages` and the whole answer as JSON in `raw_answer`. An unannounced address keeps its registry block. A `netblock` lookup now records the same: `ripestat::Routing::attributes` is the one place an answer becomes evidence attributes, so a block's record holds everything an address's does, and a block no AS announces keeps its registry block instead of being a zero.
