- Add a keyless `crypto` lookup for one Bitcoin address, Spiderfoot's `BITCOIN_ADDRESS` target. It confirms the address on-chain through blockchain.info's public `rawaddr` endpoint and reports its current balance, lifetime received and sent totals, and transaction count. Every figure is kept in full fidelity: the human-readable BTC amount in the report summary and the exact satoshi integer in the evidence. Base58 (`1…`, `3…`) and bech32 (`bc1…`) mainnet addresses are accepted; an address the chain cannot parse or does not hold is a clean negative. The lookup is reachable as `huntsman-recon crypto ADDRESS`, as `scan -k crypto` (and so from the Web UI's New Scan picker), and as a saved-scan target that round-trips through the Spiderfoot-compatible layer; a Bitcoin-address element in a result graph now offers "New Scan of this".
