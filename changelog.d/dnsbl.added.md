- `ip` and `domain` (and `scan`, `sf -s` and the web UI) ask the DNS blocklists Spiderfoot's `sfp_spamhaus`, `sfp_spamcop`, `sfp_uceprotect`, `sfp_dronebl`, `sfp_sorbs` and `sfp_surbl` ask, plus the Spamhaus DBL for domains and UCEPROTECT level 3, keyless through the device's own resolver. Each listing is kept with its list, zone, answer and reason as `BLACKLISTED_*` and `MALICIOUS_*` elements, each list its own source. A zone is trusted only after its RFC 5782 test points answer, so a refused, unreachable or answer-everything zone is reported as such, never as "not listed". `Transport::resolve` is the one system-resolver path.
